# Accessibility

ATTRITION is an early alpha. This page lists honestly what the game offers today
and where it still falls short. If something keeps you from playing, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/Vyrenbusiness-source/attrition-releases/issues/new/choose)
with the `accessibility` label.

## What the game offers

**Subtitles and radio**
* Subtitles for spoken lines and radio messages, in three sizes (small, medium, large).
* Important events (orders, call-ins, objective changes) also appear as text messages in the HUD.

**Controls**
* Every key can be rebound in *Einstellungen* (settings). The menu warns about double bindings and can reset everything to the defaults.
* Adjustable mouse sensitivity, separate options for helicopter controls.
* Basic controller support in menus and for drones.

**Display and HUD**
* Window mode and fullscreen. Tested resolutions range from 1280×720 to 2560×1440, including 21:9 ultrawide.
* Adjustable field of view.
* HUD size can be scaled, the HUD can be turned off, and individual HUD elements (hit markers, hit direction, kill feed, center dot, first-time hints) can be switched on or off.
* A **reduce motion** option for less camera shake and screen movement.
* Quality presets from Low to Ultra for older PCs.

**Audio**
* Separate volume sliders, including hit and kill sounds that can be turned off or adjusted.
* Directional hit indicators, so incoming fire is shown visually as well as heard.

## Known limitations

* **German only.** All menus, subtitles and the HUD are in German for now. An English version is planned.
* No color-blind presets yet.
* Controller support is partial. The game is built and tested for mouse and keyboard.
* No screen reader support for menus.
* Some situations depend on hearing, for example locating distant gunfire or drones. Subtitles and hit indicators help, but do not replace every sound cue.
* Combat includes realistic violence, flashing explosions and muzzle flashes at night. There is no photosensitivity mode yet.

Feedback on any of these points helps us decide what to fix first.
